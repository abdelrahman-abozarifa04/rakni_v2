Support and FAQ

If you ever need help, the Rakni team is here with quick answers and direct support.

Help center
Why is my spot not visible?

Check your filters and time range. Some locations are only visible when availability opens.

Can I change my vehicle later?

Yes. Update or add vehicles from your profile, then select the new plate before booking.

How do refunds work?

Refunds follow the cancellation policy shown during booking. Eligible refunds appear in your payment method.

Where do I find booking history?

Open the bookings tab in the app to see active and past reservations, receipts, and status updates.

Need direct help?

Reach the support team in the app or email support@rakni.app for urgent cases. Include your booking ID for faster resolution.
